Tyler Burns · 7 pieces

Dynamic Poetry

Poems that will not hold still. Each one alters itself a word or a letter at a time, so by the time you reach the bottom the top has changed, and you have to read it again.

The Old Pond · Matsuo Bashō, tr. Harry Behn

  • The Old Pond, One Night

    Dusk to dawn and round again. The moon comes down into the water, a crow takes the morning, and by daylight the pond is itself. Read again from the top.

    An old silent pond A frog jumps into the pond— Splash! Silence again.→An old silent moon A frog leaps into the moon— Splash! Silence again.
    22 versions · one or two letters per step · closed loop

Genesis · on Bashō’s pond

  • The Old Pond, Creation

    The pond runs back to the formless deep and is made again in six days, each ending on the old refrain. On the seventh, a frog. Read again from the top.

    An old silent pond A frog jumps into the pond— Splash! Silence again.→The first living soul A breath went into the man— Good! Morning again.
    52 versions · one word per step · closed loop

  • The Old Pond, the Fall

    An old Eden pond, one word from Bashō’s. A tree, a snake, a bite, and two people walking east.

    An old silent pond A frog jumps into the pond— Splash! Silence again.→An old Eden pond A hand reached into the tree— Bite! Silence again.
    50 versions · one word per step · one way

  • The Old Pond, Cain and Abel

    Two brothers, two fires, one field. The story of Cain and Abel, told without ever leaving Bashō’s haiku. Read again from the top.

    An old silent pond A frog jumps into the pond— Splash! Silence again.→An old silent field Two men went into the field— Thud! Silence again.
    69 versions · one word per step · closed loop

  • The Old Pond, the Flood

    The pond rises until it takes the world. Then a dove, a leaf, a bow in the cloud, and the only promise in the series that answers “again.”

    An old silent pond A frog jumps into the pond— Splash! Silence again.→The wide endless sea The world went into the sea— Splash! Silence again.
    54 versions · one word per step · one way

  • The Old Pond, Babel

    One speech, a plain, bricks, a tower whose top may reach unto heaven. Then the words scatter, and what is left is Bashō’s pond in five tongues at once.

    An old silent pond A frog jumps into the pond— Splash! Silence again.→The high tower’s top The Lord came into the town— Down! Silence again.
    46 versions · one word per step · one way

The Gospels · on Bashō’s pond

  • The Old Pond, the Passion

    From a cold silent night to a dark silent noon. The life and death of Jesus, told without ever leaving Bashō’s haiku.

    An old silent pond A frog jumps into the pond— Splash! Silence again.→It is finished now A breath went into the dark— Gone! Silence again.
    132 versions · one word per step · one way
